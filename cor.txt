## Never Give Profits Back! Let's Chase the Profits (Trailing Stop Loss)

🏃‍♂️ **The Survival Guide - Post 19**

A Golden Rule in trading is "Let your winners run and cut your losers short."
However, while "letting it run," the ultimate technique we use to secure the profits we've already made (Locking profits) is called a Trailing Stop Loss (TSL).

🔒 **1. What is a Trailing Stop?**
A normal Stop Loss (SL) is stuck in one place (Static). But a Trailing Stop Loss is a dynamic SL that follows the market as it moves in your favor (into profit).

* As the market goes up, the TSL also goes up.
* But if the market comes down (reverses), the TSL stays "locked" at the highest point it reached. The moment the market hits it, the trade is Closed in profit!

🪜 **2. How do we do this practically? (The Strategy):**
Although you can set Auto Trailing on MT4/MT5, professional traders do this Manually.

* Imagine you placed a Buy order. The market never goes up in a straight line. It moves like a staircase (creating Higher Highs and Higher Lows).
* As the market breaks a new High and moves up, you lift your SL and place it slightly below the previous Low (Swing Low) that was formed.
* When it forms another new High, you lift the SL to the next newly formed Low.
In this way, you can keep collecting profits until the Trend is completely over!

⚠️ **3. The biggest mistake many make (Trailing Too Tight):**
When setting a TSL, many people out of fear place the SL so close to the market price that it's "almost touching it."
You need to give the market room to Breathe. The market naturally gives small Pullbacks. If your TSL is too close, your trade will hit the SL and Close during that small pullback, and then you'll just have to watch as the market skyrockets again.

💡 **Pro Tip:**
If you find it hard to figure out exactly where to place a TSL, add the 20 EMA (Exponential Moving Average) or the ATR (Average True Range) Indicator to your chart. If you trail your SL just outside these lines, you can catch a Trend from the very beginning to the very end!

## Let's make "Free" money from the market! (Breakeven & Partials)

🆓 **The Survival Guide - Post 08**

One of the biggest Golden Rules in Trading is: "Never let a winning trade turn into a loser!"

🛡️ **1. Breakeven (BE) - "A free trade":**
Imagine you place a Buy order, and it moves beautifully in your direction (e.g., a 1:1 RR is complete).
Now, your Stop Loss is at the bottom. What you do is lift that SL and place it exactly at your Entry price! *

* **The Advantage:** What happens if the market crashes back down now? It hits your SL. But you don't lose a single cent! ($0 Loss). This is called a Risk-Free Trade. Now you can sleep without any fear.

💰 **2. Taking Partial Profits (Putting half in your pocket):**
Imagine you placed a 0.10 Lot trade and it's in profit. But you are scared the market might reverse, yet you still want to hold until your full target (TP).
What you do is Close half of your 0.10 Lot (0.05) right then and put that profit in your pocket! You let the remaining 0.05 run.

* **The Advantage:** Now you definitely have a guaranteed profit in your pocket. If the rest hits the TP, even more profit. If it reverses, no problem at all, because you already secured some money early!

🎯 **3. The Ultimate Combo of both:**
What Smart traders do when a trade goes halfway is,

1. First, they take Partials (put a portion of the profit in their pocket).
2. Then, they move the SL of the remaining trade to Breakeven (BE).
Now there is no way for you to lose! 100% win! Wherever the market goes, you are happy. 😎

⚠️ **A quick piece of advice:**
As soon as you place a trade and it goes slightly into profit, don't rush to BE in a panic. You must give the market room to fluctuate (Breathe). Otherwise, your BE will get hit and the market will continue in your predicted direction. Only use these techniques after you reach at least a 1:1 RR.

## The "Death Certificate" of the Economy! How to see a Recession 6 months in advance. (The Yield Curve)

☠️ **The Economic Masterclass - Post 15**

Over the past few days, we talked about data that has already entered the market (like Inflation, NFP).
But did you know the market has an Indicator that shows the biggest upcoming "economic earthquake" well in advance?
We call it the Yield Curve Inversion.
To understand this, let's first look at what Bonds are.

📜 **1. What is a Bond?**
Simply put, when the government runs out of money, they borrow money from the public (and investors). In return, they give us a piece of paper (Bond) saying, "In this much time, we will give you this much interest (Yield) for this."

📊 **2. The Normal Rule of the World (Normal Yield Curve):**
Imagine you lend money to the government for 2 years. You get a 2% interest.
But what if you lend money for 10 years? Because your money is tied up for a long time, the risk is higher. Therefore, the government must give you a higher interest, like 4%.
"More time -> Higher interest (Yield)." This is the normal situation.

☠️ **3. The "Death Certificate" of the Economy (The Inversion):**
However, sometimes the market "goes crazy."
Investors become extremely fearful about the immediate future (1-2 years) (due to something like a war or bank collapse).
Then they take all their money and put it into 10-Year Bonds for safety.
What happens then?
The interest on 2-year bonds becomes higher than the interest on 10-year bonds!
"2-Year Yield > 10-Year Yield"
We call that situation a Yield Curve Inversion.

📉 **4. What happens if this occurs?**
Throughout history, every single time this has happened, a Recession has inevitably followed within the next 6 to 18 months.
❗️ Banks stop lending.
❗️ Companies close down.
❗️ The Dollar crashes.

💡 **What do you do as a Trader?**
If you see that the Yield Curve has Inverted, never take a Risk in the market!
During that time, money flows into Safe Havens. That means things like Gold (XAUUSD) and the Japanese Yen (JPY) skyrocket.
