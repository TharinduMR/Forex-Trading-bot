## The meter that measures the "Fuel" of a Trend! (What is Open Interest?)

🧠 **Sentimental Analysis Masterclass - Post 10**

When traders globally open a new trade (Buy or Sell), this Open Interest value increases. When they close their trades and exit the market, this value decreases.
For a vehicle to move forward, it needs continuous fuel, right? For a market to keep moving up or down, it needs continuous "new money" (Open Interest) added to it!

📊 **How to catch Fake Trends using this?**
There are 4 very simple rules:

🟢 **1. Price ⬆️ + Open Interest ⬆️ = 🚀 (Extremely strong Up Trend):**
The price on the chart is going up, and the OI value is also increasing.

* **Meaning:** Big whales are pouring billions in new money to push the market up. This is a very strong trend. Buy without fear!

⚠️ **2. Price ⬆️ + Open Interest ⬇️ = 🛑 (Fake Up Trend / Reversal):**
The chart goes up, but the OI value decreases!

* **Meaning:** No new money is entering the market. The price is only rising because people are closing old short trades (Short Covering). The vehicle is out of fuel! The market could crash very soon. Do not Buy!

🔴 **3. Price ⬇️ + Open Interest ⬆️ = 💥 (Extremely strong Down Trend):**
